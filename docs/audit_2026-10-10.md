# PHBW8 historical audit (2026-10-10; corrected 2026-10-11)

This was a read-only audit of the existing PHBW8 work under
`/workdir/naj49/g2f_assemblies_2024/`. No files were modified, moved or
deleted. Contig statistics were recomputed from the sequence-free GFAs
(`*.noseq.gfa`, using the `LN:i:` tags).

The audit is split into four parts:

* **Verified**: read directly from software output or logs.
* **Interpretation**: our reading of the verified facts. These are not
  conclusions.
* **Unresolved**: questions that remain open.
* **Actions**: proposed follow-up work.

The register of legacy runs is in `docs/legacy/PHBW8_legacy_runs.yaml`.

## 2. Interpretations (hypotheses, not conclusions)

* **Coverage estimates.** hifiasm's early k-mer estimate (37×) and its later
  read-level estimates (72/36) come from different stages and may not mean
  the same thing. This is **recorded as an unresolved observation**. It is not
  evidence that coverage estimation or purging went wrong. A controlled
  PHBW8 sensitivity test (e.g. `--hom-cov`) should be considered only if
  independent evidence (Merqury k-mer spectra, read depth over contigs)
  justifies it.
* **Default-run haplotype output.** Whether the 365 Mb hap2 and the ~35 Mb
  removed by default purging are real redundancy, collapsed repeats or
  artefacts is **untested**. We make no claim that the default result is
  worse.
* **ONT contribution.** Adding ONT (≥ 50 kb) cut the contig count from 1,492
  to 490 and shortened the primary assembly by 48.7 Mb compared with
  HiFi-only `-l0`. The largest contigs and the N50 contig changed little.
  What the removed or merged sequence was has not been characterised.
* **BUSCO duplication (16.6–17.3%)** is consistent with maize's ancient
  genome duplication. It is not used as evidence of haplotig retention.
* **The three runs are benchmarks, not a ranking.** Choosing between them
  needs independent QC: Merqury QV and k-mer completeness, read-depth
  support, collapsed or extra sequence, and organelle content.

## 3. Unresolved questions

1. **Source HiFi BAMs.** The location and checksums of
   `m84109_240806_024441_s4.hifi_reads.bc2027.bam` and
   `m84109_240611_191050_s2.hifi_reads.bc2088.bam` are unknown.
2. **BAM-to-FASTA conversion.** The tool, version and command are unknown.
   We have not verified that both movies are in `EB2_PHBW8.hifi.fasta.gz`;
   the workflow's full movie census will establish it.
3. **ONT source.** The raw ONT files, the total unfiltered yield, the
   filtering tool and command, and whether the cutoff was "> 50 kb" or
   "≥ 50 kb" are all unknown.
4. **Exact commands.** The GFA-to-FASTA commands are unknown; the FASTAs were
   written 3–7 hours after the GFAs. The BUSCO command lines and whether BUSCO
   ran from `~/busco.sif` or `/programs/busco-6.1.0` are also unknown.
5. **Coverage estimates.** How hifiasm's early and later coverage estimates
   relate (section 2) is unresolved.
6. **Internal stop codons.** Why 127 complete BUSCOs contain internal stop
   codons is unknown. Possible causes include assembly base errors,
   pseudogenes, miniprot alignment behaviour, and lineage or maize-specific
   effects. Merqury QV will help separate these.
7. **Naming.** Historical files use both `PHBW8` and `EB2_PHBW8`.
   Proposed: `PHBW8` as the canonical ID, `EB2_PHBW8` as an alias.

## 4. Proposed actions

* Register the three runs as legacy benchmarks without moving or modifying
  them (done: `docs/legacy/PHBW8_legacy_runs.yaml`).
* Re-run PHBW8 through the new workflow. This gives full read QC, checksums,
  a movie census, Merqury, and BUSCO with `poales_odb12` as the primary
  lineage. Comparing it with legacy run 3 tests reproducibility.
* Later, run Merqury and `poales_odb12` BUSCO on legacy runs 1 and 2 as well,
  so the benchmarks are compared on the same metrics.
* Locate the source BAMs and raw ONT reads. If found, prefer them as inputs
  so that raw yields and checksums are recorded.
* Investigate the internal-stop-codon BUSCOs once Merqury QV is available.
* Screen small contigs for organelle and contaminant sequence (they are
  listed by the workflow).
